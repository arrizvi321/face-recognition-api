I tested three facial recognition models using Deepface library in Python: SFace, ArcFace and FaceNet51. 

SFace is the fastest and takes 2-3 seconds.
ArcFace takes about 7-8 seconds, but has the highest seperation from pictures of me
compared to pictures of Alok. 
FaceNet51 takes the longest (around 10 seconds), has same seperation as SFace, but
highest accuracy.

SFace seems better because it is the fastest and produces correct results. ArcFace 
seperates between two people better while FaceNet51 has the strongest same-person closeness.
